# Getting started

Zevont gives your team a workspace for planning projects, tracking work, and designing systems.

1. Sign in to [Zevont](https://dev-hub.site).
2. Create an organization or join one from an invitation.
3. Create a project and open **Workshop**.
4. Choose the tool you need. Your organization role controls which tools you can edit.

[Browse the Workshop tools](/workshop/) or [build your first ERD](/erd/).
